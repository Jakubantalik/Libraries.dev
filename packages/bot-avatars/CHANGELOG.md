# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release: ten types (clover, flower, triangle, square, blob, ghost, circle, drop, star, robot), two faces (eyes, mouth), four states (default, thinking, happy, sleeping) with head turns, hops, flips and cross-animated switches, palette colours with `color`, `brightness`, `saturation` and `ink` overrides, `size`, `speed`, `paused`, `seed`, `shading` (per-pixel plastic by default, crisp, smooth or flat) with `shadow`, `highlight`, `light`, `depth`, `rim` and `spread`. Eyes and head follow the pointer, a click hops and flips (`interactive`). Rendered on a 2D canvas as a lit extrusion; the rig and renderer are exported.
