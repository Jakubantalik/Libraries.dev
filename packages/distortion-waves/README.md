# Distortion waves

A colourful distortion wave that opens from a button, a screen edge or an
avatar and carries one screen into another: a quick pull toward the source, a
soft swell with progressive blur and chromatic aberration, and colour that
stays on the edges, with a hairline on the glass, after the middle has
cleared.

Three types:

- **Vertical** — up the screen from a button at the bottom.
- **Horizontal** — across the screen from a tall source at the left.
- **Center** — a ring out from an avatar or icon, round a sheet or card.

SwiftUI only for now (iOS 17+): [`ports/ios/DistortionWavesKit`](ports/ios/DistortionWavesKit).
Try it on [libraries.dev/distortion-waves](https://libraries.dev/distortion-waves),
and tune every option in the Studio.
