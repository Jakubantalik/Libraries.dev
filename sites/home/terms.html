The team MCP server

Business includes an MCP server that connects your team's coding agents to your brand kit, your team's Studio presets and the Pro references. Each member connects with their own token from npx libraries-dev mcp; it is personal to that seat, must not be shared or committed to a repository, and stops working when the member leaves the team or the plan ends. Presets a member saves, in the Studio or through the server, and the brand kit are visible to everyone on the same team. The server does not run an AI model for you: your own agent does the work, under its provider's terms.

The Studio agent and its monthly allowance

The Agent tab sends your request to an AI model that tunes, or rebuilds, the effect for you. Its use is measured in credits, which track the model usage a request costs: a simple tuning request uses about one credit, and a request that rebuilds an effect's core can use up to about twenty. Use is limited per account per calendar month. On Pro: 300 credits and at most 150 agent requests. On Business, per seat: 800 credits and at most 400 agent requests. On a lifetime plan: 100 credits and at most 50 agent requests a month, up to 3,000 credits in total over the life of the account. Once that total is used the agent stays off for the account, while the rest of the Studio keeps working. This allowance is not part of what the lifetime payment buys; a subscription or Business seat on the same account gets that plan's allowance instead. When a monthly limit is reached the agent stops answering until the first day of the next month, while the manual controls, presets and everything else in the Studio keep working. Unused allowance does not carry over, is not refundable, and is not a quota you own — we may change the limits, and the model behind the agent, as the Beta evolves. The agent may also decline requests unrelated to the effect in front of you.

Code the agent writes (a rebuilt core) runs in your browser and, if you export it, in your app; it is provided as-is under No warranty below, and it is yours to use under Using the libraries above.
